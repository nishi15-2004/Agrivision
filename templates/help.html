❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check the weather?

Open the Weather section and search for your city. You can also use the current-location option if location access is enabled.

How can I detect a crop disease?

Open Disease Detection, upload a clear crop-leaf image and submit it for analysis.

Where can I find government schemes?

Open Government Schemes to view important agricultural schemes and access their official government portals.

How can I check crop-loss compensation?

Open Compensation and select the type of crop damage to view relevant information and available support.

How can I check mandi prices?

Open Market Prices, select a commodity and optionally select state, district and mandi to search available market data.
